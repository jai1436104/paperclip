# NaadiOS Operating Company

This package models NaadiOS as a Paperclip company.

The active operating core is designed for the next 3 to 12 months:

- product completion and launch readiness
- operator-console stewardship and clinic onboarding
- testing, security, and infrastructure planning
- GTM, sales, and scenario-based finance
- wiki-backed memory and research synthesis
- survival-first decision making

The package also includes a parked vision branch so longer-horizon platform ideas stay captured without hijacking current execution.
